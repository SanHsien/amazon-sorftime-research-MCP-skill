**Option 2: Technical Troubleshooting**

- Try a different outlet to rule out power source issues
- For APP issues, try reinstalling the app or clearing the cache

**Option 3: Refund or Return**
If you prefer not to troubleshoot, we are happy to:
- Process a full refund (no need to return the heavy item)
- Send you a completely new unit
- Offer a partial refund as compensation

Please let us know which option you prefer, and we'll take care of it immediately. We value your business and hope to restore your confidence in our brand.

## 亞馬遜合規注意事項

### ✅ 推薦做法

1. **使用官方渠道**: 透過亞馬遜後臺 "Contact Buyer" 功能傳送
2. **快速響應**: 收到差評後24小時內處理
3. **態度誠懇**: 承認問題，不推卸責任
4. **提供解決方案**: 補發、退款、技術支援
5. **請求反饋**: "如果解決方案讓您滿意，請考慮更新評價"

### ❌ 嚴禁做法

1. **不要直接請求刪除評價**:
   - ❌ "Please remove your review"
   - ❌ "Change your review to 5 stars"

2. **不要用利益交換評價**:
   - ❌ "I'll give you $20 for a 5-star review"
   - ❌ "Free gift if you delete your review"

3. **不要使用威脅性語言**:
   - ❌ "If you don't remove this, we'll ban you"

### ✅ 建議的委婉表達

- "If you're satisfied with our solution, we'd appreciate it if you could update your feedback to reflect your improved experience."
- "We hope we've earned back your trust. Your updated review would help other customers see our commitment to customer service."
